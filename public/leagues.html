    How to Play

    ×
    • Each week, pick the winner of 5 games from that week's matchups.
    • Choose one of your 5 picks as your Bonus Pick.
    • A correct pick earns 10 points. A correct bonus pick earns 10 points + that team's final score.
    • An incorrect pick, or a tied game, earns 0 points.
    • Your bonus team must be different every week.
    • You must submit all 18 weeks before the season begins. Once submitted, picks can't be changed.
